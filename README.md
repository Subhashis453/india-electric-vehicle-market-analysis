# ⚡ Indian EV Market Analysis – AtliQ Motors Expansion Strategy 🇮🇳🚗

"The Electrics are not just vehicles, they're a movement to make our planet a better place."

Welcome to the comprehensive data-driven Indian Electric & Hybrid Vehicle Market Analysis done for AtliQ Motors – a US-based EV giant aiming to dominate the Indian market.

This project was commissioned by Bruce Haryali, Head of AtliQ Motors India, and led by Peter Pandey's data analytics team to assess the viability of launching AtliQ’s best-selling EV/Hybrid models in India.

🎯 Goal: Provide data-driven insights to guide product launch, brand strategy, and market penetration.

Key Business Insights

🔝 Top EV Makers: Ola, TVS, Ather

📉 Bottom EV Makers: Kinetic Green, Jitendra, Battre Electric

📈 States with Highest Growth: Karnataka, Goa, Kerala, Delhi

🏭 Ideal Manufacturing State: Maharashtra (due to subsidies, infrastructure, and governance)

📆 Peak Sales Month: March (291K units)

🛑 Low Sales Month: June (103K units)

📢 Suggested Brand Ambassador: Virat Kohli – Pan-India appeal & eco-conscious image
